Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

More buyers now ask an AI tool first — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot, and Gemini — instead of scrolling a list of links. AEO helps those tools find your business, understand it correctly, and quote and cite you in their answers.

It is SEO for AI answers: the same discipline as getting found on Google, aimed at the answer itself. TechMind builds it the same way we built it for our own business-loans site — the same setup, applied to your business.

  • AI crawler access — your site opened to the crawlers that power AI answers (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and others), set in robots.txt
  • Sitemap — submitted and kept up to date, so AI tools and search engines find every page
  • llms.txt — a short, plain guide to your business that AI tools can read and quote from
  • Structured data (schema) — your business, services, and FAQs marked up so machines read them unambiguously
  • Answer-first pages — pages that answer one customer question clearly in the first sentences, so an AI can cite them
  • Citation tracking & reporting — we check what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini actually say about you, and report which questions cite you

What AEO is not

  • No guaranteed citation, ranking, or placement. No one can guarantee an AI will cite you — the engines decide. We remove the obstacles and prove results with real citations, not promises.
  • No fake mentions. No invented reviews, no fake listings, no misleading claims about your business.
  • Not a one-time trick. AI answers change week to week, so AEO is measured and maintained, not set and forgotten.

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank your site in a list of links. AEO aims to get AI tools — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini — to quote and cite your business inside their answers. The work overlaps (crawlable pages, clear answers, structured data), but the goal is a citation, not just a click.
